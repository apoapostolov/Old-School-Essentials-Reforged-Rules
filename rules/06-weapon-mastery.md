# Weapon Mastery

## Weapon Mastery

Weapon Mastery is an optional system adopted from the Rules Cyclopedia with
modifications. It replaces the RC's Weapon Proficiencies (deliberately
excluded). Fighters retain the ability to use all weapons; mastery represents
dedicated training beyond basic competence.

### System Overview

Weapon Mastery has 5 ranks. __Basic (BS)__ is your baseline proficiency: if
your class allows a weapon, you are automatically BS in it at no slot cost.
Each earned weapon slot advances one weapon by one rank:

| Rank | Slots Spent (Beyond BS) | Attainable Via |
| --- | --- | --- |
| __Basic (BS)__ | 0 (baseline, free) | Class weapon allowance |
| __Skilled (SK)__ | 1 | Level slots or training |
| __Expert (EX)__ | 2 | Level slots or training |
| __Master (MS)__ | 3 | Level slots (Fighter + Other Warriors) or training |
| __Grand Master (GM)__ | 4 | Level slots (Fighter only) or Training (all others) |

__Hard rule__: Basic is not a purchased rank: it is your starting
proficiency. The Fighter gains 4 level-granted slots by 14th level, allowing
them to reach Grand Master in one weapon (4 slots beyond Basic) through
levelling alone. Other Warrior classes gain 3 slots (Master at L12), and
Adventurers gain 2 (Expert at L10). Mages receive no weapon slots:
spellcasters improve their weapons through the Training system or not at all.
Any rank beyond your level-granted slots requires the Weapon Training system.
The Fighter's unique privilege is Grand Master at 14th level: the only class
to achieve it without a master.

### Slot Progression by Level (OSE 14-Level Game)

All characters begin at 1st level with Basic mastery in their class-allowed
weapons (Fighters and demihumans: all weapons; other classes: as per class
weapon allowances). No spendable slots at L1.

| Class | Slots Gained At | Total Slots | Max Rank (No Training) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| __Fighter__ | L4, L8, L12, L14 | 4 | Grand Master (4 slots) at L14 |
| __Other Warrior__ (Dwarf, Elf, Barbarian, Duergar, Half-Orc, Knight, Paladin, Ranger) | L4, L8, L12 | 3 | Master (3 slots) at L12 |
| __Adventurer__ (Cleric, Druid, Drow, Thief, Halfling, Acrobat, Assassin, Bard, Half-Elf) | L5, L10 | 2 | Expert (2 slots) at L10 |
| __Mage__ (Magic-User, Gnome, Illusionist, Svirnefblin, Sage) | - | 0 | Basic only (train for all ranks) |

__Example: Fighter progression__ (4 slots total):

- L1: Basic in all weapons (Fighter bonus). 0 slots.
- L4: 1 slot: Skilled in longsword.
- L8: 2 slots: Expert in longsword.
- L12: 3 slots: Master in longsword.
- L14: 4 slots: __Grand Master__ in longsword. Familiarity Bonus not yet
  active (only 1 weapon at Skilled+).

__Example: Fighter with Familiarity Bonus__ (the versatile path):

- L1: Basic in all weapons. 0 slots.
- L4: Skilled in longsword (1 slot).
- L8: Expert in longsword (2 slots).
- L12: Expert in longsword + Skilled in longbow (3 slots). Familiarity active.
- L14: Master in longsword + Skilled in longbow (4 slots). Needs training for
  Grand Master.

_The GM path makes you the deadliest swordsman in the land. The Familiarity
path makes you dangerous with anything you pick up. Neither is wrong.*

*_Example: Dwarf progression__ (3 slots total):

- L1: Basic in all weapons (demihuman bonus). 0 slots.
- L4: Skilled in battle axe (1 slot).
- L8: Expert in battle axe (2 slots).
- L12: Master in battle axe (3 slots). Enjoys two full levels of Master-tier
  combat (Sweep, Deflect 3) before the campaign ends.
- To reach Grand Master: must find a GM-rank teacher and complete the trial:
  the campaign epilogue quest.

__Example: Magic-User progression__ (0 slots):

- L1: Basic in staff and dagger (class weapons). 0 slots: and never gains any.
- If the player wants Skilled in staff: find a Skilled+ staff instructor (any
  town guard captain), pay 500 gp, train 2 weeks, pass STR check.
- If the player wants Expert: find an Expert+ instructor, pay 2,000 gp, train
  4 weeks, pass STR check at -2.
- This is intentional: spellcasters invest gold and downtime in weapon skill
  the same way warriors invest in spell scrolls: it is secondary, earned, and
  never free.

__Fighter bonus__: Fighters begin with Basic mastery in __all__ weapons at no
slot cost. Their earned slots advance weapons beyond Basic. This preserves the
Fighter's "master of all weapons" identity while allowing deep specialisation.
At 14th level, with 4 slots in one weapon, the Fighter attains Grand Master:
the only class to do so through levelling alone.

__Demihuman bonus__: Elves, Dwarves, and Halflings also begin with Basic
mastery in all weapons their class normally allows.

__Familiarity Bonus__: A character with at least Skilled rank in two or more
different weapon types gains +1 to hit with ALL weapons (including unarmed and
improvised). This represents the cross-training insight of a versatile weapon
master. The bonus applies once regardless of how many weapons are Skilled+.

### Weapon Training (Attaining Ranks Beyond Level Slots)

Characters may seek out masters to train beyond what their level-granted slots
allow. This is the __only__ path to Grand Master, and a viable path for any
rank for characters who want to accelerate or diversify their mastery.

#### Finding a Teacher

| Desired Rank | Teacher Must Have | Where to Find Them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic to Skilled | Skilled+ in that weapon | Any town guard captain, mercenary sergeant, or veteran adventurer |
| Skilled to Expert | Expert+ in that weapon | Town+; knightly orders, fighting schools, military academies |
| Expert to Master | Master+ in that weapon | City+; renowned weapon masters, retired champions, elite military |
| Master to Grand Master | Grand Master in that weapon | __Quest-gated__. There may be only 1-3 GM practitioners of a given weapon in the entire Duchy of Karameikos. Finding one is an adventure in itself. |

__Locating a teacher__: Spend 1d4 days asking around in a suitable settlement.
CHA check: on success, you find a lead. On failure, no teacher of that rank is
currently available here: try a larger settlement or a different region.

__GM teachers__: Finding a Grand Master is not a downtime activity: it is a
quest. The DM should prepare: the master's name, location (a remote monastery,
a hidden mountain dojo, a retired hermit), their price (which may not be
gold), and a test they require before accepting a student.

#### Training Costs and Time

| Training | Time | Gold Cost | Ability Check | On Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BS to SK | 2 weeks | 500 gp | STR (melee) / DEX (missile) | Lose 1 week and 250 gp; may retry immediately at half time/cost |
| SK to EX | 4 weeks | 2,000 gp | STR/DEX at -2 | Lose 2 weeks and 1,000 gp; must wait 1 month before retrying |
| EX to MS | 8 weeks | 5,000 gp | STR/DEX at -4 | Lose 4 weeks and 2,500 gp; must find a different teacher or prove yourself anew |
| MS to GM | 16 weeks | 15,000 gp | STR/DEX at -6 | Lose 8 weeks and 7,500 gp; the master will not teach you again: find another |

__Ability check__: Roll d20 equal or under (STR or DEX + modifiers). Reaction
may open the door, but it cannot replace aptitude. Modifiers:

- Teacher is friendly or impressed by you: +1
- Teacher is indifferent: +0
- Teacher is reluctant or you failed their test: -1
- Natural 1: automatic success, regardless of modifiers. Your epiphany during
  training impresses the master.

__Partial progress__: Unlike the RC original, failure does not mean total
loss. You retain partial progress (the time and gold lost represent wasted
effort, but you learned something). The "On Failure" column shows the actual
cost.

#### Quest and Reputation Requirements

| Rank | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| BS to SK | None. Gold and time suffice. |
| SK to EX | The teacher must respect you. Provide evidence of combat experience (a trophy, a tale that holds up to scrutiny, a demonstration bout). |
| EX to MS | The teacher requires a __service__: retrieve a rare weapon component (e.g., griffon feather for fletching), defeat a rival student in a formal duel, or craft a masterwork weapon as your training project. |
| MS to GM | The teacher requires a __trial__: defeat them in a non-lethal bout (they fight at -4 to hit, you must land 3 hits before they land 5), retrieve a legendary weapon manual from a dangerous location, or defend the master's school against a threat. The trial is a mini-adventure, not a single roll. |

__Reputation__: After completing MS to GM training, the character's name
becomes known in martial circles. +1 Reaction from warriors, mercenaries, and
knights who recognise the achievement. The character may be challenged by
rivals seeking to prove themselves against a Grand Master (1-in-6 chance per
visit to a major city).

#### Training Slots

A training slot is earned each time you successfully complete a training arc.
It is a permanent bonus slot dedicated to the weapon you trained in. Training
slots do not count toward the level-granted slot cap and may exceed it freely.

__Maximum training slots__: A character may earn 1 training slot per weapon.
You cannot train the same weapon twice: once you have earned a training slot
for a weapon, further improvement in that weapon must come from level-granted
slots (up to Master) or you are already at Grand Master.

### Rank Benefits (All Weapons)

Each rank provides cumulative benefits:

| Rank | To Hit Bonus | Damage | Deflect | Special |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| __Basic__ | +0 | As weapon table | 0 | - |
| __Skilled__ | +1 | As weapon table | 1 | See weapon-specific specials |
| __Expert__ | +2 | As weapon table | 2 | Special improves |
| __Master__ | +4 | As weapon table | 3 | Primary + Secondary target damage |
| __Grand Master__ | +6 | As weapon table | 3+ | Best special; primary/secondary targets |

Hit bonuses are compressed compared to the original RC tables (+0/+1/+2/+4/+6
instead of +0/+2/+4/+6/+8). The 8-point gap between Basic and Grand Master
created a "GM or go home" dynamic. The compressed curve keeps the damage
scaling and specials as the primary incentive for deep investment while
narrowing the hit gap from 40% to 30%.

__Deflect__: At the start of each round, before initiative, a character with
Deflect X may declare they are deflecting. They roll Xd6. Each result of 4-6
deflects one melee or missile attack that would hit them that round. Deflected
attacks deal no damage and trigger no on-hit effects (poison, energy drain,
etc.). A character using Deflect cannot also attack that round (they are
focused on defence). Maximum attacks deflected per round = Deflect value.

__Deflect fatigue__: Each consecutive round of Deflect beyond the first
imposes a cumulative -1 die penalty to the Deflect dice pool (Deflect 3: 2
dice on second round, 1 die on third). After 3 consecutive rounds of Deflect,
the character is fatigued (-1 to all rolls until a rest turn is taken).

__Primary/Secondary targets__: Some weapons (polearms, two-handed swords,
battle axes) can strike multiple adjacent foes. Primary target takes full
damage; each secondary target takes the listed secondary damage. Maximum
secondary targets = 2 at Master, 3 at Grand Master.

#### Unarmed Combat Styles

Unarmed combat has three distinct mastery tracks, each requiring separate
weapon slots. Fighters get Basic free in Brawling, Wrestling, and Improvised
Weapons. __Martial Arts is the exception__: no class receives Basic in it for
free. It must be unlocked through the Martial Arts skill (see Skills) or by
finding a teacher through the Weapon Training system. Other classes may train
in them individually through the Weapon Training system.

### Special Effects Glossary

| Effect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| __Stun__ | Target saves vs __Paralysis__ or loses next action. Cannot move or attack for 1 round. Already-stunned targets are not re-stunned. |
| __Disarm__ | Target saves vs __Breath__ (reflex: snatch weapon back) or weapon flies 1d10' in random direction. Natural 1 on save = weapon broken. |
| __Staggered__ | Target acts at initiative 0 next round (last in order). No save. Caused by bow GM hits and battle axe GM. Replaces the old "Delay" and "slowed" terminology. |
| __Lingering Wound__ | When a creature is reduced to 0 hp by a Slow weapon, it does not die until the end of the round (it may take one attack action). At GM Battle Axe: extends to the end of the NEXT round. This is the Slow weapon death-delay rule, clarified here. |
| __Brace vs Charge__ | When set against a charge, deal double damage on hit. Must declare before initiative; cannot move that round. |
| __Sweep__ | Attack primary target normally. Each secondary target in adjacent squares takes secondary damage (no separate attack roll needed: use same roll at -2). |
| __Riposte__ | 1/round, when an enemy misses you in melee, make a free counterattack at -2. Damage: as weapon table but one die smaller (d8 to d6, d6 to d4, etc.). |
| __Piercing Bolt__ | (Crossbow GM only) On a hit, the bolt passes through the target. Free attack vs next creature in line of fire at -4. Chains up to 3 targets. |
| __Impale__ | (Lance GM foot only) On natural 19-20, target is stuck on the lance: grappled, takes 1d4 damage per round until they spend an action to pull free. |
| __Grapple__ | (Wrestling BS) On hit, target is held: cannot move, -2 AC vs others. __Escape (opposed roll-under STR)__: Both grappler and target roll d20 equal or under their STR. Compare margins: (a) both fail: stalemate, hold maintained; (b) only one succeeds: that side wins; (c) both succeed: wider margin (lower roll relative to stat) wins. Natural 1 = critical, auto-win. Grappler cannot use a weapon while maintaining the hold but may use Wrestling techniques. |
| __Pin__ | (Wrestling SK) As Grapple but target cannot act and grappler cannot move. Escape uses the same opposed STR check, but the target must succeed by 3 or more points of margin over the grappler to break free. |
| __Throw__ | (Wrestling EX) Release a held/pinned target to hurl them 1d4×5', dealing 1d6 damage and knocking them prone on landing. |
| __Joint Lock__ | (Wrestling MS) Held target takes 1d2 subdual damage per round automatically until they escape. No action required from the grappler. |
| __Submission__ | (Wrestling MS) When a held target is below 1/2 hp, you may force a Morale check at -2. On failure: target surrenders. |
| __Choke Hold__ | (Wrestling GM) Pinned target begins suffocating: CON rounds until unconscious. Each round: opposed roll-under STR check. If target wins, choke broken but still pinned. If grappler wins, suffocation continues. Natural 1 on escape = target slips free entirely. |
| __Open Hand__ | (Martial Arts EX) On natural 20, target saves vs Paralysis or stunned AND pushed 5' back. The push is automatic even if the stun is saved. |
| __Flurry__ | (Martial Arts GM) Make 2 attacks per round with unarmed strikes. The second attack is at -2 to hit. |
| __Unarmoured Defence__ | (Martial Arts SK/MS/GM) AC bonus when wearing no armour. Stacks with DEX modifier. Lost if any armour is worn or encumbrance exceeds 400 cn. |

### Retraining Weapon Mastery

Between adventures: 2 weeks + 100 gp × character level. Reduce one mastered
weapon by 1 rank and recover the slot. You cannot reduce below Basic if the
class starts with Basic in all weapons.

### Mastery Interactions

Weapon Mastery hit bonuses stack with all other modifiers (surprise, darkness,
spells, etc.). Deflect is resolved __after__ the attack roll is compared to
AC: if the attack would hit, the defender rolls Deflect dice. A deflected
attack deals no damage and triggers no on-hit effects (poison, energy drain,
etc.). Mastery bonuses do not apply to spells or spell-like abilities: only
to physical attacks with the mastered weapon.
