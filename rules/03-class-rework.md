# Class Rework

## Class Rework

## Basics of Class Rework

Classes are broken into three distinctive groups:

- __Basic Classes:__ These include the Cleric, the Fighter, the Magic User, and the Thief. __All Basic Classes have the standard Primary Attribute Requirements of 9.__ With minor improvements to their role, these classes are left unchanged.
- __Demihuman Classes:__ These include the Drow, the Dwarf, the Duergar, the Elf, the Gnome, the Halfling, the Half-Elf, the Half-Orc, and the Svirnefblin. __Their primary Attribute Requirements are all increased to 11.__ These classes are rebalanced with a boost of their unique abilities over human characters. Any class from a third party supplement that is a demihuman class follows the same rules and is a subject of improvement by the DM, if needed.
- __Advanced Classes:__ These include the Acrobat, the Assassin, the Barbarian, the Bard, the Druid, the Illusionist, the Knight, the Paladin, and the Ranger. Advanced classes have class-specific requirements (typically one attribute at 13+ and a secondary at 9+). See the requirements table below. Any non-basic class from a third party supplement follows the same rules and is a subject of improvement by the DM, if needed.

For each class, combine the write-up in the __Old School Essentials Advanced Player's Tome__ with the changes and additions listed here.

## THAC0 / ToHit progression

Attack roll use the formula: __d20 + Attack Bonus + STR (melee) or DEX (ranged) modifirt ≥ target’s Ascending AC__. These progressions __replace all class THAC0 tables__. They smooth original B/X advances into gradual +1 steps (never more than +1 per level), matching exact final bonuses at traditional caps. Demi-human classes plateau after their max levels but retain the bonus beyond.

## Basic Classes

### Cleric

- __Cure Disease (ritual):__ The Cleric can use holy water and prayer to alleviate and stop the spread of disease. The Cleric has a 4-in-6 chance to cure simple diseases and a 2-in-6 chance to cure simple virulent diseases; the check can be repeated daily for normal diseases, or weekly for virulent diseases. The cleric has a 1-in-6 chance to attempt to cure magical or unholy diseases or potent diseases with no known or simple cure; if they can do so, they must use their Turn Undead vs a disease with an HD value decided by the DM (for example, the Black Plague is a 5 HD disease). If Turning is successful, the disease stops developing, and another successful Turning on another day is needed to cure it. If Destruction is successful, the disease is cured. If cured, the patient recovers in 3d6 days after receiving the cure, or half that if they are believers of the same faith or alignment.
- __Holy Sense:__ The Cleric can concentrate for one turn on a held item or on an area to tell if it is magical by divine means and whether the item is holy, unholy, or a divine artifact of any alignment, and its properties if the item is holy based on their beliefs. The Cleric can also concentrate on a 30-foot area and tell if it is consecrated or desecrated based on their beliefs. The Cleric can reveal divine artifacts, items made by divinely aligned creatures, intelligent items with an alignment, or cursed items (although the specifics of the curse cannot be revealed).
- __Faith's Influence:__ The Cleric's recognized faith grants them authority in social situations, but only with those beneath them in station or order. When making a Reaction check, or a Wisdom or Charisma check against a target who recognizes the Cleric's religion and is not in an authoritative position (such as a peasant, a lower-ranking priesthood member, or a younger acolyte), the Cleric gains +1 to the check. If the target shares the same faith and alignment, the bonus increases to +2. Targets actively hostile to the Cleric's faith may instead suffer a similar penalty at the DM's discretion.

### Fighter

- __Cleave:__ The Fighter can cleave through multiple enemies in melee range. On their turn, when the Fighter reduces an enemy to 0 hp with a melee attack, they can make one additional melee attack against another opponent they are engaged with. They learn to cleave twice at 5th level, three times at 9th level, and four times at 13th level. At 9th level, the Fighter can spend one use of cleave to move no more than 10 feet and engage another enemy.

### Magic User

- __Arcane Sense:__ The Magic User can concentrate on a held item for one turn to tell if it is magical by arcane means and its properties. Cursed items never reveal their curse until the item is used correctly. If the item is intelligent, the Magic User may confirm that and evaluate a vague approximation of the intelligence (below, at par, or above theirs).

> 👉🏼 The Magic User’s ability to detect magic items does not make the __Read Magic__ spell useless. The spell is cast in a round; it identifies things that are not held items, such as terrain and hidden magical traps, and has no limit on the number of items it can detect. Still, the __Read Magic__ spell does not identify an item’s properties.

### Thief

- __Skill Progression:__ Thief Skills use a modified version of the Optional Thief rules in Carcass Crawler #1 (pg. 28) but converted to support percentile values as supported by the Foundry system.
  - At Level 1, the Thief has a value of 10% in all its skills (25% in Climb) and 1-1 in its Hear Noises Skill. The Thief then distributes 4 increases between these skills. Each increase adds +15% to the skill's percentile value or +1 to the lower value of an X-in-6 for Hear Noises. You may not put more than two increases in the same skill.
  - At gaining a new level, the Thief can distribute two additional increases. You may not put more than one increase in the same skill.
  - Percentile skills cannot exceed a maximum of 85%; value reached with 5 increases. The Hear Noises skill cannot exceed a maximum of 5-in-6; the value reached with 4 increases.

> 🔦 __Environmental Edge:__ The Thief may increase their chances in a percentile skill by +10 or +20 if they use environmental factors such as shadows and light sources or engage in competence roleplay of situations where wrong decisions and poor use of real-life knowledge may cause failure.

## Demi-human Classes

The minimum attribute requirement for all demi-human classes is 11.

### Drow

- The Drow racial die is d6.
- __Dark Assassination:__ The Drow is trained in the art of assassination in darkness. When attacking in pitch darkness, the Drow benefits from +4 to Attack Roll against unaware or blinded creatures. However, when attacking, the Drow may forfeit this bonus and deal double damage on a successful hit.
- __Dex Finesse (Drow arms):__ The Drow is trained in dark-elven combat techniques that emphasize speed and accuracy. When they use weapons made by Drow, they can choose to have their Dexterity Bonus, rather than their Strength Bonus, added to the Attack and Damage Rolls.
- __Poisoncraft (spiders):__ The Drow can spend a day in the company of friendly poisonous giant spiders of 1 HD or more and produce 1d3 uses of Bloodstream Poison Type I or Ingested Poison Type II. For each 3 HD above the first in spider hit dice, increase the type of poisons by one type category. The Drow cannot repeat the process with the same spider population in a week.
- __Sleep/Paralysis Immunity:__ The Drow's __Immunity to Ghoul Paralysis__ has been changed to __Immunity to Sleep and Paralysis.__ Spells or creature attacks cannot put the Drow to sleep or paralyze them. However, they can be petrified by creatures such as Basilisks or Medusas.
- __Sylvan Sense:__ The Drow can concentrate for one turn on a held item to determine its magical status by arcane means but cannot reveal its properties; only a Magic User, Illusionist, and sometimes a Cleric can do this.

### Dwarf

- __Stout Fortune:__ Once per day, the Dwarf can reroll a failed saving throw versus Death or Poison, Paralysis or Petrification, or Spells (but not Rods and Staves).
- __Hardy Growth:__ The Dwarf gains +1 hit point every time they level up after 1st level. The Dwarf rolls their hit dice twice when leveling up and chooses the higher result (and can still reroll a result of 1 or 2 once).
- The Dwarf has __Attack Giant Foes__. Against giant opponents (larger than human-sized), they benefit from +2 to attack rolls.

### Duergar

- __Iron Will:__ Once per day, the Duergar can reroll a failed saving throw versus Poison or Spells.
- __Stone Stealth:__ Duergar's Stealth applies to all stone-made interiors with exposed rough stonework, not just underground.
- __Harm Giant Foes:__ Against giant opponents (larger than human-sized), they benefit from +2 to damage.

### Elf

- __Low-Light Vision:__ The Elf loses __Infra-vision 90'__. Among elves, it is available only to the Drow. The Elf gains Low-Light Vision (see __Low-Light Vision__).
- The Elf’s __Arcane Magic__ grants it access to the spell lists of both the Magic User and Illusionist. The Elf also gains two additional Beginner Spells, rolled as normal.
- __Elven Finesse:__ The Elf is trained in elven combat techniques that emphasize speed and accuracy. When using weapons made by elves, the Elf can choose to have their Dexterity Bonus, rather than their Strength Bonus, added to the Attack and Damage Rolls.
- The Elf’s __Detect Secret Doors__ is changed to __Detect Secrets__. The Elf has a 2-in-6 chance to detect hidden objects when actively searching. The Elf’s cunning intuition also has a 1-in-6 chance of knowing whether someone is keeping a secret from them, even without fact-checking. However, the Elf does not automatically know what the secret might be.
- __Sleep/Paralysis Immunity:__ The Elf's __Immunity to Ghoul Paralysis__ is changed to __Immunity to Sleep and Paralysis.__ The Elf cannot be put to sleep or paralyzed by spells or creature attacks. However, they can be petrified by creatures such as Basilisks or Medusas.
- __Sylvan Sense:__ The Elf can concentrate for one turn on a held item to determine its magical status by arcane means but cannot reveal its properties; only a Magic User, Illusionist, and sometimes a Cleric can do this.

### Gnome

- __Blink Away:__ Once per day, when the Gnome takes damage from an enemy, they may become invisible until the start of their next turn or until they receive damage again before their next turn.
- The Gnome's __Arcane Magic__ allows them to choose between the Magic User and Illusionist spell lists. The choice is made upon character creation and cannot be changed later.
- __Terrain Hiding:__ The Gnome has a 4-in-6 chance to Hide in a densely forested environment, a 3-in-6 chance to hide in a crowded urban environment, and a 2-in-6 chance to Hide in a natural dungeon or furnished interior environment, and 1-in-6 chance if none of these apply. As long as enough cover is available, the Gnome can hide and remain hidden while they take no action. The chances increase by 1 if done in pitch darkness or under moonlight. The Gnome can remain hidden for 1d3 rounds; it must repeat the check to extend the period by an additional 1d3 rounds.

### Halfling

- __Lucky:__ The Halfling is born lucky. Once per day, they can reroll a failed saving throw or a d6 check to Hide (see below).
- __Stout Heart:__ Halflings gain +2 to saving throws versus magical effects that charm, dominate, possess, or compel action against their nature. This stacks with their racial save bonuses.
- __Terrain Hiding:__ The Halfling has a 5-in-6 chance to Hide in a densely forested environment, a 4-in-6 chance to hide in a crowded urban environment, and a 3-in-6 chance to Hide in a natural dungeon or furnished interior environment, and 1-in-6 chance if none of these apply. As long as enough cover is available, the Halfling can hide and remain hidden while they take no action. The Halfling can remain hidden for 1d3 rounds; it must repeat the check to extend the period by an additional 1d3 rounds.
- __Underfoot Defense:__ Creatures larger than human-sized suffer -1 to hit the Halfling in melee.

### Half-Elf

- __Low-Light Vision:__ The Half-Elf loses __Infra-vision 90'__, among all elves it is available only on the Drow. The Half-Elf gains Low-Light Vision (see __Low-Light Vision__)
- __Mixed Finesse:__ The Half-Elf is trained in elven combat techniques that emphasize speed and accuracy. When using weapons made by elves or humans, the Half-Elf can choose to have their Dexterity Bonus, rather than their Strength Bonus, added to the Attack and Damage Rolls.
- __See Through Pretense:__ Living between two cultures grants the Half-Elf keen insight into deception. The Half-Elf has a 1-in-6 chance to passively detect lies, disguises, or hidden motives during conversation, no action required.
- __Keen Awareness:__ Half-Elves are wary observers, rarely caught off guard. When the party is surprised, the Half-Elf rolls 1d6 and is only surprised on 1-3 (50% chance to act normally during surprise rounds).
- __Diplomat:__ The Half-Elf is a born diplomat. The Half-Elf gains +1 to Reaction Rolls from all creatures that do not bear hatred towards humans or elves. Once per encounter, if a Morale check is called during parley or negotiation and the situation is not outright hostility (e.g., attack immediately), the Half-Elf may reroll that Morale check and must take the new result.
- __Sleep/Paralysis Immunity:__ The Half-Elf's __Immunity to Ghoul Paralysis__ is changed to __Immunity to Sleep and Paralysis.__ The Half-Elf cannot be put to sleep or paralyzed by spells or creature attacks. They can, however, be _petrified_ by such creatures as Basilisks or Medusas.
- __Sylvan Sense.__ The Half-Elf can concentrate for one turn on a held item to determine its magical status by arcane means but cannot reveal its properties; only a Magic User, Illusionist, and sometimes a Cleric can do this.

### Half-Orc

- __Low-Light Vision:__ The Half-Orc gains Low-Light Vision (see __Low-Light Vision__)
- The Half-Orc racial die is d6.
- __Grim Tenacity:__ The Half-Orc's hardiness defies death. When reduced to 0 hp, the Half-Orc must succeed on a saving throw versus Death to remain conscious; on success, they may act until the end of the next round or until reduced to -10 hp. On failure, they drop and begin dying as normal.
- __Stubborn Vitality:__ When Half-Orcs reduce their Constitution when they reach 0 hp or lower, they reduce by that amount minus one. On 5th and 9th level, the reduction increases by one.
- __Brutal Grapple:__ The Half-Orc's unarmed attacks and unarmed combat techniques, such as grappling, deal 1d4 damage in addition to their other effects. However, the Half-Orc can choose not to deal this damage.

### Svirnefblin

- __Stone Camouflage:__ When motionless against a stone surface underground, the Svirnefblin has a 4-in-6 chance to be completely unnoticed by creatures without infra-vision or tremorsense. This combines with their Blend into Stone ability for extraordinary stealth in their native environment.
- __Gem Sense:__ The Svirnefblin can concentrate for one turn on a gem or precious stone to evaluate its approximate value and detect if it has been magically altered or is an illusion. The Svirnefblin can also sense the presence of gem veins within 30 feet, though this only confirms their presence and provides no information about their type, quantity, or value. This builds on their innate Stone Murmurs ability.
- __Sure-Footed:__ The Svirnefblin takes half the damage from falling on a rocky or earthy surface. When a Svirnefblin starts falling, and there's a rock edge in reach, they may attempt to save vs. Breath attack and grab a rocky edge on success, avoiding a fall.

### Acrobat

- Requirements: DEX 13+, STR 9+
- __Acrobat Skills__ use a percentile system modeled on Thief skills.
  - At Level 1, the Acrobat begins with the following values: Climb Sheer Surfaces 40%, Falling 25%, Tightrope Walking 40%, Hide in Shadows 10%, and Move Silently 10%.
  - At Level 1 the Acrobat does not distribute increases. At gaining a new level, the Acrobat can distribute two additional increases. You may not put more than one increase in the same skill. Each increase adds +15% to a chosen skill.
  - Climb Sheer Surfaces and Tightrope Walking may reach 100%. Falling, Hide in Shadows, and Move Silently may reach 70%.
- Once per day, the Acrobat can reroll a failed saving throw versus Breath or versus Spells, Wands and Staves as long as they are unimpeded to move, and are not surprised by the effect.
- __Dodge Die:__ Once per round, if an Acrobat is about to take damage from a successful attack, they roll a d4 and add the rolled result to their AC. If this causes the AC to become higher than the attack roll, the attack still hits but does only 1 hp of damage and no other effects, if any. The die size changes to d6 at the 5th level, d8 at the 9th level, and d10 at the 13th. The Acrobat does not benefit from Dodge die if they are prone, grappled, or their movement is impeded or slowed by external factors such as difficult terrain.
- __Tumbling Strike:__ When the Acrobat attacks, they make an FA skill check before the attack roll, or they can announce a Charge to make a tumbling attack. On success on the FA check or using a Charge, the attack, if successful, does double damage, and if the opponent is unaware of the attack, the attack benefits from +4 to hit. On failure of the FA check, the attack, if successful, makes only 1 point of damage (no damage roll is made), and the Acrobat cannot use a tumbling attack until the end of the encounter.

### Assassin

- Requirements: DEX 13+, INT 9+
- __Assassination Scaling:__ The damage dealt when the target of an assassination succeeds on its save against instant death is doubled on the 5th, tripled on the 9th, and quadrupled on the 13th level. For each level above 9th, the target's save against instant death check result is decreased by 1.
- __Skill Track (Thief conv.):__ The Assassin refers to the Thief's custom rules for Climb, Hear Noises, and Move Silently. No change is made to Assassination skill.
  - The Assassin’s Climb skill matches the values of the Thief.
  - The Assassin starts with 2 increases at level 1. They get 1 increase on each level up.

### Barbarian

- Requirements: STR 13+, CON 9+
- __Skill Track (Wilderness):__ Skills use a percentile system modeled on Thief skills.
  - At Level 1, start at: Climb 25%, Hide in Undergrowth 10%, Move Silently 10%.
  - No increases at 1st level. Levels 2-9: __+1 increase per level__. Levels 10-14: __+2 increases per level__. Each increase is __+15%__ to a chosen skill. Only 1 increase per skill per level.
  - __Caps:__ Climb 85%; Hide in Undergrowth 70%; Move Silently 70%.
  - Result: exactly 18 total increases by 14th level; full caps are only reachable at high level, not midgame.
- __Damage Reduction:__ The Barbarian reduces all damage taken by non-magical attacks by 1. This reduction increases to 2 at the 9th level. The damage taken cannot be reduced below 1.
- __Battle Senses:__ The Barbarian has sharpened all its senses in combat. When caught surprised, unaware, or blind-fighting, such as in pitch darkness, the benefit to hit enemies receive is reduced to +2 instead of +4. When surprised or in pitch darkness, the Barbarian can make a saving throw against Death to prevent damage against them from being doubled.
- __Charge Fury:__ When the Barbarian charges, in addition to +2 to the attack roll, they also benefit from +2 plus their Strength bonus for the damage roll. The Barbarian can also charge again after they reduce an enemy to 0 hp.

### Bard

- Requirements: CHA 13+, DEX 9+
- __Bard Skills__ reflect the Bard's subtlety and performance.
  - At Level 1, the Bard begins with Pick Pockets 10%, Move Silently 10%, Hide in Shadows 10%, and Hear Noise 1-in-6.
  - The Bard starts with 1 increase at 1st level. Starting with Level 2, the Bard gains 1 increase each level. Each increase adds +15% to a percentile skill or +1 to Hear Noise (max 5-in-6). Only 1 increase per skill per level.
  - Pick Pockets, Move Silently, and Hide in Shadows can reach 70%.
  - Hear Noise can reach 5-in-6.
- __Battle Songs:__ When rolling initiative, a Bard may choose to forgo movement this round and sing, or forgo making an attack this round and play an instrument. If they sing during the round, allies benefit from +2 to Morale Checks. If they play an instrument during the round, enemies that can hear the music suffer -2 to their attack rolls unless they attack the Bard.
- __Stunning Flourish:__ When the Bard charges and hits, they may choose to do 1 point of damage instead of rolling for damage. If they do, the target must save versus spell or be stunned, losing their next turn. Until they act again, attacks against the target benefit from +2 to hit.
- __Bardic Knowledge.__ The Bard can concentrate for one turn on a subject, an object, or a person and has a 1-in-6 chance of knowing an obscure fact about them. There is an additional 2-in-6 chance that the fact will be helpful for the party and its goals, and on a roll of 6 on that secondary check, the fact is always wrong. The fact is never a direct solution or applicable hint to what actions the party must follow. Both checks are made by the DM, and the second roll is never revealed to the player. The first check improves to 2-in-6 at the 5th, 3-in-6 at the 9th, and 4-in-6 at the 13th level.

### Druid

- Requirements: WIS 13+, CHA 9+
- __Favored Terrain Vow:__ The Druid has a favored terrain, the region's wilderness, which they have vowed to protect. With the blessing of their circle, a Druid may travel to discover new terrain in distress that they can vow to protect once they accomplish a deed to defend it.
- __Herbal Salves:__ The Druid can make natural salves that stop bleeding and ease pain as long as they are within or have access to their favored terrain. The Druid can recover 1d3 hp to self or another living creature, and double the natural healing rate for the day. Each creature can benefit from this once per day.
- __Antivenom Craft:__ The Druid can use survival skills and natural means to cure weaker poisons. The Druid has a 5-in-6 chance to cure poison. The chance is lowered by 1-in-6 for each level of poison above level I. A chance of 0-in-6 makes it impossible to attempt a roll.
- __Wild Bonds:__ The Druid suffers -4 to Morale checks to recruit and maintain service of non-animal retainers and hirelings, but +2 to Morale checks to encounter and recruit animal companions. Druids can calm animals and beasts (including dire beasts) with a +2 bonus to the Morale Check. Druids can be uncooperative at times and have difficulty communicating with civilized folk, and civilized folk often have a negative bias towards them.
- __Animal Companion:__ The Druid may start with an animal companion and recruit as many animals as possible up to their hireling limit. The animal companion should be native to their favored terrain, be treated as a henchman, and count against the limit. They should not make Morale checks (or use a Morale value of 10 if needed) or require anything beyond care and companionship. Each animal may have HD no higher than a third of the Druid's HD, rounded up. If lost, a replacement animal companion can be found within 1d4 weeks per HD of the lost companion, provided the search occurs in the Druid's favored terrain. The DM decides the number of HDs of the companion using bestiary entries rather than the theoretical maximum allowed by these rules. If the Druid gains levels that change their companion’s Hit Dice limit, the animal companions grow in experience along them, with additional Hit Die added to their stats.

### Illusionist

- Requirements: INT 13+, DEX 9+
- __Minor Conjurations:__ The Illusionist can spend one hour conjuring an illusion of a small object or tiny creature that appears natural at a cursory glance. The Illusionist must be intimately familiar with a real object or creature to create an illusion of it. It has a 3-in-6 chance to behave as expected, a 2-in-6 chance to feel genuine to touch, and a 1-in-6 chance to emit sound or smell that resembles the real thing; these are rolled when the illusion is created. As long as the illusion appears natural, any other creature needs to interact with the illusion (usually by touching it) and make a simple Wisdom check to disbelieve it for themselves. The illusion cannot deal damage or interact with other creatures that are not illusions; it is destroyed if any damage is dealt. The illusion appears as a magical effect to spells that detect magic. All illusions disappear when their creator loses consciousness (such as sleeping or being knocked out). The number of minor illusions an Illusionist can maintain at once is 2 plus their Intelligence modifier.
- __Illusion Savvy:__ Illusionists do not need to interact with an illusion; if they can see it, they can make a simple Wisdom check to disbelieve it for themselves (others are still subject to believe in it).
- __Arcane/Illusion Sense:__ The Illusionist can concentrate on a held item for one turn to determine if it is magical by arcane means and its properties. Cursed items never reveal their curse until they are appropriately used. The Illusionist can also focus on an item or area of up to 30 feet and sense if it is an illusionary object or location as long as the level of the illusion spell used to cast the illusion (or the maximum spell level available to its caster if not via a spell but a ritual or other grand magic of permanence) is equal or lower than the maximum spell level available to the Illusionist. For example, a 5th-level Illusionist can see through illusions cast by a slot up to the 3rd level and ritual illusions of other magic users and Illusionists of the 6th level and below.

### Knight

- Requirements: STR 13+, CHA 9+
- __Noble Standing:__ The Knight's social status cannot be lower than a landless Noble. If the player rolls for social status and does not like the result, they can become a landless Noble instead. The Knight also has a claim to an inheritance that the DM may interweave in their personal campaign goals; if the inheritance is claimed, the 9th-level keep becomes immediately available at the current level.
- __Retinue:__ The Knight can take two extra hirelings, and an additional one at 5th and 9th level. The Knight benefits from +2 to attract retainers and hirelings, +2 to interview them, and +1 to recognize their capabilities and statistics.
- __Shield Stand:__ The Knight can announce a shield stand before rolling initiative each round; it precludes movement this turn. The Knight's benefit from using a shield and all adjacent allies wielding a shield increases from +1 to +2 to AC and +1 to Parry attack rolls until the start of the next turn.
- __Battle Oath:__ Once per encounter, the Knight can speak a passionate oath of the moment before they roll initiative on a round of their choice. This oath may taunt opponents of opposite alignment to focus their attacks on the Knight. Until the Knight remains standing, all hirelings have +2 to their Morale Checks.

### Paladin

- Requirements: CHA 13+, WIS 9+, must be of Lawful alignment
- __Donation Zeal:__ The Paladin gains one and a half times the XP reward for donated gold and treasure. While 10% is the required minimum by their church, there is no maximum amount the Paladin can donate.
- __Dedication to Law & Good.__ The Paladin has a +1 to attack rolls against inherently evil creatures with hit dice equal to or greater than theirs. They also deal +2 extra damage when they hit an inherently evil creature with hit dice less than theirs.
- __Smite Evil:__ At 5th level, when the Paladin rolls a natural 20 on an attack roll against an inherently evil creature, the attack deals maximum damage plus an extra damage roll. At 9th level, the Paladin smites on a natural 19, and at 14th level, the Paladin smites on a natural 18.
- __Divert the Wicked:__ The Paladin can taunt evil creatures able to reach and attack the Paladin within their next turn to draw their attention away from non-Paladin allies. High-intelligence evil creatures require a properly worded taunt to draw them to attack the Paladin against their interests.
- __Sanctified Sense:__ The Paladin can spend a turn concentrating to detect the presence of evil enchantments and creatures of evil alignment within 60'. Unlike the detect evil spell, this does not cause them to glow; it only gives the Paladin a confirmation of nearby presence. The Paladin can also hold an item or an intelligent weapon and sense if it is inherently evil or tainted by a greater evil.
- __Clean of Body:__ The Paladin is immune to common diseases. A common disease is a non-magical disease with a known cure or a chance of survival without a cure. The Paladin has a 2-in-6 chance to cure simple diseases and a 1-in-6 chance to cure simple virulent diseases with a lay of hand; the cured patient recovers in 3d6 days.

### Ranger

- Requirements: STR 13+, WIS 9+
- __Favored Terrain:__ The Ranger has a favored terrain, the wilderness of the region where they have been born or have lived most of their lives. The chosen favored terrain cannot be changed. They have trained all their life to protect the wilderness against two groups of common enemies: in temperate woods, these are goblinoids and orcs. The DM may change one or both of these groups if the common enemies of other terrains differ.
- __Common Enemies:__ Two enemy groups tied to the favored terrain (e.g., goblinoids, orcs in temperate woods).
- __Terrain Skills__ are tied to the Ranger's favored terrain.
  - At Level 1, the Ranger begins with Hear Noise 1-in-6, Hide in Shadows 10%, and Move Silently 10%.
  - The Ranger starts with no increases at level 1. Starting with level 2, the Ranger gains 1 increase each level. Each increase adds +15% to a percentile skill or +1 to Hear Noise (max 5-in-6). Only 1 increase per skill per level.
  - Hide in Shadows and Move Silently can reach 70%.
  - These skills function only in the Ranger’s favored terrain.
- __No Tithes:__ The Ranger's requirement to donate has been removed. The only class required to donate is the Paladin, and they have additional benefits from that.
- __Fleet in Terrain:__ The Ranger's walking speed in their favored terrain increases by 10 feet when not impeded by armor, rugged terrain, or other circumstances.
- __Enemy Slayer:__ The Ranger has vowed an enmity against all races threatening their favored terrain, usually the woods. The Ranger may perform an assassination (similar to the Assassin class) against their common enemies, gaining +4 to attack rolls and dealing double damage if the enemies are unaware. They also have +1 to the damage rolls against their common enemies (does not stack with weapon specialization).
- __Vigilant Guide:__ The Ranger is always vigilant in their favored terrain, and when not distracted by any other tasks, they lower the party surprise chance, usually 2-in-6, to 1-in-6. If the Ranger spends an hour to create a hideout in their favored terrain, they can increase the enemy surprise chance, usually 2-in-6, to 3-in-6.
- __Rough Company:__ Due to their uncouth nature, the Ranger suffers -2 to Morale checks when recruiting and maintaining service for non-animal retainers, and humanoid hirelings who do not live in or near their favored terrain. They have +2 to Morale checks when encountering animals and beasts (but not dire beasts, those only the Druid can calm) common to their favored terrain.
